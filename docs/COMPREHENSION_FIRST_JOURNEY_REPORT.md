## Fresh and returning learners

A fresh profile opens directly into `g2-story-scouts-prose`, `ss-unit-1`, difficulty 1, with no Start screen or selector. Existing compatible comprehension sessions retain normal resume priority. A Word Forge active session encountered before the later word-study stage is suspended only after an authoritative save, with no completion, failure, reward, or mastery event. Its bounded response state and all durable Word Forge history remain available for exact validation when Word Forge becomes eligible.

The deferral transition is idempotent. It holds at most one session, does not persist authored text, and does not erase plans or reviews merely because they are temporarily ineligible. Stale Word Forge plans cannot pull the learner ahead of an unfinished comprehension stage.

## Opening Story Map experience

Story Map content version `g2-ss-plot-elements-r0.2.0` uses three distinct checkpoint stories:

- `Story Map Checkpoint: Tia's Cleanup Plan`: Tia organizes neighbors to clean a littered street before wind spreads the trash.
- `Story Map Checkpoint: The Steady Bridge`: Luis replaces a rushed weak bridge model with a carefully measured design.
- `Story Map Checkpoint: Harper Saves the Seedlings`: Harper responds to hard rain by protecting and restoring a class garden bed.

Each checkpoint keeps one stable two-paragraph source for all six questions. The response set covers an explicit problem, cause, character actions, resolution Hot Text, beginning/problem/resolution table mapping, and a supported inference with paired evidence. Every selectable segment and evidence reference is owned by that story. No spelling, vowel, or isolated sound question appears in these checkpoints.

The earlier seventh question in each checkpoint is retired rather than hidden. The Story Map pack now has seven lessons, seven texts, thirty-eight active questions, and twenty-eight support targets. Current registry totals are 40 packs, 280 lessons, 294 texts, 1,611 questions, and 1,111 support targets. Grade 2 has 886 questions; Grade 3 remains unchanged at 725.

## Progression and scoring

The strong threshold remains 85 percent, two distinct independent activities remain required, and assistance remains excluded from independent evidence. On a six-question checkpoint, five correct is 83.33 percent and therefore does not qualify as strong. A successful first checkpoint selects a distinct verification; a successful second proof advances Story Scouts under the existing progression rules.

Comprehension verification, remediation, review, recycling, and active-session recovery remain skill- and unit-affine. A low Story Scouts result cannot route into Word Forge. Word Forge reviews remain queued and regain eligibility when the later word-study stage is reached.
