## Clarifications

- Perspective means a character’s particular attitude toward or way of regarding something.
- Point of view identifies the narrator; the scored skill distinguishes character perspective from narrator point of view without classifying the narrator.
- Supporting details may come from words, actions, feelings, choices, and noticing.
- The pack focuses on a best-supported character perspective rather than comparing multiple themes or analyzing perspective development.
