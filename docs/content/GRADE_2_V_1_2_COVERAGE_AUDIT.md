## Behavior notes

- Base words are identified before the affix meaning is combined.
- Prefixes are taught as coming before the base word.
- Suffixes are taught as coming after the base word.
- Whole-word meaning is derived from the base word plus the affix.
- Transparent composition is required; spelling-change forms are excluded.
- No Greek-root claim is made.
- No Latin-root claim is made.
- No context-clue claim is made.
- No learner-mastery claim is made.
- No human-approval claim is made.
