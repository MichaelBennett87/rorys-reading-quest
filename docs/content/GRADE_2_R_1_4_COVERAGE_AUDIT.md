## Sound-versus-spelling behavior
- Rhymes are judged by sound.
- End words must match the authored rhyme families.
- Different spellings may still rhyme when the sound matches.

## Capital-letter notation behavior
- The first rhyme family is labeled A.
- Repeated rhyme families reuse the same letter.
- New rhyme families receive the next unused uppercase letter.
- The full sequence of letters names the rhyme scheme.
