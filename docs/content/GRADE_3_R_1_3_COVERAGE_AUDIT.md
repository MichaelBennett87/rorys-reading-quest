## Behavior audit

The curriculum teaches a perspective as how a character sees or understands a shared event, not as a feeling word, trait, narrator position, or author viewpoint. Learners identify each character's words, actions, thoughts, feelings, noticing, choices, wants, and motivations; compare similar, different, and partly similar views; and trace supported changes after plot events.

Every guide and scored comparison uses source evidence for both characters. Four guides model clearly different views, two model partly similar views, one models clearly similar views, and three records trace perspective change. Evidence and explanations remain passage-owned and DRAFT.

This is curriculum coverage, not learner mastery, human approval, Florida approval, official assessment, a FAST prediction, or a global Grade 3 diagnosis. `ELA.3.R.1.4` remains planned for Phase 7B4.
