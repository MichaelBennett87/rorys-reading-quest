The authored feature set includes titles, headings, captions, accessible labeled illustrations, glossaries, timelines, and sidebars. Every guide explains how its features contribute to navigation, clarification, sequence, comparison, or causal understanding rather than merely naming a feature.

Chronology records were reviewed for genuine time or step order. Comparison records contain meaningful similarities and differences between two subjects.
