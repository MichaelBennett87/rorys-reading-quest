# Grade 2 R.2.3 Coverage Audit

- Benchmark reference: `ELA.2.R.2.3`
- Benchmark wording summary: explain an author's purpose in an informational text
- Benchmark skill summary: identify the author's specific purpose from whole-text clues
- Review status: `DRAFT`
- Coverage status: implemented
- Contributing pack: `g2-information-detectives-purpose-path`

## Grade 2 boundary

- The pack asks for the author's specific purpose.
- The pack keeps the answer tied to the whole informational text.
- The pack avoids a generic `to inform` answer.

## Grade 3 boundary

- The pack does not ask how the author's purpose develops.
- The pack does not require rhetorical analysis.
- The pack does not move into opinion, argument, or persuasion scoring.

## Purpose evidence behavior

- Purpose evidence resolves through the shared passage-evidence contract.
- Checkpoint passages use clues from more than one section.
- Purpose evidence remains local to the passage and is not persisted to learner progress.

## Specific-purpose behavior

- Each authored passage has one specific purpose statement.
- Each statement is more specific than `to inform`.
- Each statement stays distinct from the topic and from central-idea language.

## No opinion claim

- The benchmark audit does not claim author-opinion mastery.
- The pack does not evaluate whether a reader agrees with the author.

- Coverage is authored DRAFT coverage only.
- Coverage does not claim a child has mastered the benchmark.
