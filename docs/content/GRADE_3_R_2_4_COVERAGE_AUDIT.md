## Boundary audit

- Topic-only phrases are not accepted as claims.
- Neutral central ideas are not accepted as claims.
- Author-purpose statements are not accepted as claims.
- Neutral facts are classified as possible evidence, not automatically as claims.
- Unsupported preferences are not treated as strong evidence-based claims.
- Reasons explain why a claim makes sense; evidence supports a reason.
- Circular claim restatements are not accepted as evidence.
- No rhetoric, bias, source-credibility, counterargument, persuasive-writing, vocabulary, Grade 4, or FAST-timing construct is added.

All forty-one questions use learner-visible authored text, have one defensible keyed response under their scored surface, and pass the permanent truth and evaluator contracts.
