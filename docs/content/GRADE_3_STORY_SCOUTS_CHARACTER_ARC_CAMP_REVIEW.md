## Evidence review

- Beginning evidence: every arc records an initial action, dialogue, thought, feeling, choice, or response.
- Middle evidence: every arc records a plot event and a response that begins or reveals a shift.
- Ending evidence: every arc records a later action or choice that differs from the beginning response.
- Turning points: all turning-point IDs resolve to the owning passage.
- Action evidence: present in all 9 arcs.
- Dialogue evidence: present in all checkpoint arcs and across the pack.
- Thought evidence: present in all checkpoint arcs and across the pack.
- Plot linkage: every guide includes a nonempty statement explaining how plot events caused or revealed development.
- Development summaries: each summary connects at least two stages and states an at-first, after/later, and by-the-end relationship.

## Static trait versus development review

Every scored development item distinguishes a changing response from a static trait. Trait-only distractors such as “Mina is careful” or “Priya is organized” are plausible descriptions but do not explain development across plot stages. No trait-only statement is keyed as a complete answer to a development prompt.

## Question and answer review

All 41 questions received an independent visible-content solution, authored-key comparison, and adversarial alternative review. Each key is supported by its displayed story. Hot-text sentences come from the owning passage. Beginning-middle-end tables use unique stage mappings and one unused minor-detail distractor. Two-part evidence choices directly support their Part A development explanations.

Seven draft table items were corrected before release by adding a genuinely unused minor-detail distractor required by the hardened `use_each_once` evaluator contract. No answer key, prompt, explanation, evidence reference, story sentence, or evaluator logic required correction.

## Explanation and evidence review

- Every question has a child-friendly explanation.
- Every evidence reference resolves inside the owning passage.
- Explanations describe why the keyed response traces development rather than merely restating answer position.
- No explanation names absent wording or another passage.
- No prompt provides its transfer answer.

## Word Help and accessibility review

Each passage has exactly four DRAFT Word Help targets. Focus parts reconstruct the word, authored reading and speech chunks are nonempty, and source sentences resolve. Word Help does not expose a development summary or question key. The five-stage interface remains Look at the Pattern, Break It Apart, Hear the Parts, Hear the Word, and Hear the Sentence. No Blend It control, external speech service, microphone, or audio requirement was added.

The existing semantic headings, keyboard controls, visible focus, touch targets, non-color status text, neutral selection state, green correct state, crimson incorrect state, and reduced-motion behavior remain in force.

## Safety, originality, and boundaries

The seven stories are original app-authored DRAFT content. They do not use protected characters, commercial worksheets, copied FAST passages, private child details, real private locations, traumatic situations, or shame-based behavior correction.

Character Arc Camp does not claim theme-development, perspective, narrator-point-of-view, poetry, summary, author-craft, moral-judgment, oral-reading, or FAST-prediction coverage.

## Remaining concerns

Human educational review remains required before any content review status can advance beyond DRAFT. Browser speech quality may vary, but no question depends on hearing browser speech.
