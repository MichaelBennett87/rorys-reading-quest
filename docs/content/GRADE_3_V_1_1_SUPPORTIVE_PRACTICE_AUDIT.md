## Why the status is supportive practice

The learner recognizes meanings, selects precise academic words, identifies appropriate and inappropriate uses, and chooses authored speaking or writing frames. The app does not collect or score spontaneous speech, pronunciation, original sentences, paragraphs, or unrestricted responses. These activities can support curriculum progression but cannot establish independent productive speaking or writing mastery.
