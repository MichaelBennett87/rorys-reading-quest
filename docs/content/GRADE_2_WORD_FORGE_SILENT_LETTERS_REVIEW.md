## Bounded sound review

- `kn` and `wr` are taught as quiet starting groups.
- `mb` is taught as a quiet ending group.
- `gh` is treated as a variable family with bounded examples.
- the island-family silent `s` is treated as a reviewed exception group.
- false-pattern words such as `cough`, `laugh`, `enough`, `rough`, `tough`, `number`, `timber`, `member`, and `bamboo` are excluded from silent-letter scoring.

## Displayed-chunk and spoken-chunk review

- Displayed chunks keep the written letters visible.
- Spoken chunks omit quiet letters or encode the reviewed spoken form.
- Each support target reconstructs its surface word from authored chunks.
