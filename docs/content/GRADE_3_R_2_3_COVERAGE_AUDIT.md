## Boundary

The pack asks why an author created a specific informational text and how selected facts, examples, organization, sections, and evidence accomplish that precise purpose. It distinguishes purpose from topic, central idea, generic intent, and argumentative claim. It does not activate Claim and Evidence Court, score unrestricted writing, infer learner mastery, or mark content APPROVED.
