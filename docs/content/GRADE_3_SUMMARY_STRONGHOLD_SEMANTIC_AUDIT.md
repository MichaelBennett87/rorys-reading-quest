# Grade 3 Summary Stronghold Semantic Audit

## Scope

The audit covered all 7 sources, 7 guides, 41 scored questions, 28 Word Help targets, evidence references, explanations, evaluator payloads, and bounded two-part answer matrices.

## Literary findings

- Problems and goals are explicit and summary-worthy.
- Important events advance the plot and remain in defensible sequence.
- Resolutions are preserved without invented outcomes.
- Themes are complete thoughts supported by character choices and outcomes.
- Theme-only distractors are not accepted as complete literary summaries.
- Decorative setting, object, and side-action details are safely omitted.

## Informational findings

- Topics and central ideas are distinct.
- Model summaries combine central ideas with relevant support.
- Description, cause/effect, comparison, and mixed relationships remain accurate.
- Narrow examples and side facts do not replace essential relationships.
- Central-idea-only distractors are not accepted as complete informational summaries.

## Question truth and ambiguity

Every visible option and hot-text segment was challenged against learner-visible source evidence. Multiselect cardinalities are exact, table rows have one defensible mapping, and each two-part dependency directly supports Part A. No summary candidate is equally defensible with the key. No prompt leaks the answer, no evidence crosses source ownership, and no question adds outside information.

Deterministic semantic issues: 0. Ambiguity blockers: 0. Evaluator defects: 0. One non-scored Word Help source-ownership defect was corrected before final fingerprint generation.
