## Authored split review

The pack uses authored splits such as `ta | ble`, `ma | ple`, `ap | ple`, `can | dle`, `puz | zle`, and `han | dle`. Final consonant-`le` chunks remain intact, and the splits are stored explicitly rather than generated at runtime.

## One-syllable silent-`e` exclusion review

False targets such as `sale`, `mile`, `hole`, `rule`, `pale`, and `smile` are excluded from scored consonant-`le` work.

## Irregular-word review

No irregular word is the sole basis of correctness. When a less common word appears, the surrounding context and authored split keep the answer defensible.

## Dialect-sensitivity review

The pack avoids dialect-dependent correctness. Consonant-`le` items use stable, child-friendly words with clear authored splits.
