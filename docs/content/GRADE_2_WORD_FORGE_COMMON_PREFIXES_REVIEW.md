## Base-word review

Each scored prefix target preserves a clear base word. The pack avoids opaque or historical prefix analyses, and it does not treat every initial letter match as a transparent prefix word.

## False-prefix exclusion review

Opaque starts such as `uncle`, `under`, `return`, `present`, `distance`, and `mistake` are excluded from scored prefix work.

## Spelling-boundary review

The authored display chunks reconstruct the surface words, with the prefix chunk first and the base word preserved after it. `misspell` keeps the doubled `s` at the prefix boundary.

## Pronunciation review

The words are chosen for stable, child-friendly pronunciation and do not rely on dialect-sensitive correctness. Sentence context helps confirm the intended meaning.

## Answer-choice review

Choices are unique within each item, correct answers resolve deterministically, and correct-answer positions vary across the pack.

## Explanation review

Each question includes a concise explanation that stays inside the prefix lesson boundary and does not reveal hidden answers in the teaching block.

## Evidence-reference review

Each question points at a valid passage, sentence, or word-pattern reference and resolves deterministically.

## Word-help review
