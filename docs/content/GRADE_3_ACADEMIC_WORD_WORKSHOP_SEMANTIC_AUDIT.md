## Cross-subject and semantic review

Science, mathematics, reading, writing, social studies, engineering, and project-presentation contexts are represented. At least three parts of speech occur across checkpoint work. Each checkpoint assesses meaning, appropriate use, speaking support, writing support, transfer, and misuse or near-neighbor judgment without requesting open response.

The blind review found no required vocabulary substitution. Twelve bounded corrections were applied before final registration. One source possessive, one key/source interpretation mismatch, one defensible distractor, and several tightly scoped frame, context, misuse, and Word Help metadata issues were corrected. Four questions received explicit correction provenance. No evaluator defect required a change to scoring thresholds, rewards, review intervals, or persistence.
