## Boundary checks

- Topic versus central idea stays distinct.
- Central ideas are complete thoughts, not topic labels.
- Stated central ideas resolve to explicit body sentences.
- Inferred central ideas are identified from relevant details.
- Relevant details can come from more than one section.
- Scored tasks identify central ideas and relevant details, but do not explain how details support the central idea.
- No learner-mastery claim is made.
- No human-approval claim is made.
- No Grade 3 explanation-of-support claim is made.
- No author's-purpose claim is made.
- No author's-opinion claim is made.
