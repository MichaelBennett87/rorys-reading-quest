## Construct audit

- Topic: taught as the broad subject, never accepted as a complete central idea.
- Stated central idea: represented by three texts with resolved explicit evidence.
- Implied central idea: represented by four texts, including two checkpoints, with combined section evidence.
- Relevant detail: treated as a fact, example, observation, or explanation that develops the central idea.
- Minor detail: treated as a true but nonessential fact rather than false information.
- Summary: distinguished from central idea as a brief account containing the central idea plus major details.
- Section contribution: every guide explains how each section adds support to the whole-text idea.
