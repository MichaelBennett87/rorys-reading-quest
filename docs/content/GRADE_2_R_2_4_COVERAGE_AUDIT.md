# Grade 2 ELA.2.R.2.4 Coverage Audit

## Benchmark reference

- `ELA.2.R.2.4`

## Benchmark wording summary

- Explain an author's opinion or opinions and supporting evidence.

## Grade 2 opinion-and-evidence boundary

- Teach identifying an author's opinion.
- Teach identifying more than one authored opinion when a passage contains them.
- Teach distinguishing fact from opinion.
- Teach distinguishing opinion from topic, central idea, and author's purpose.
- Teach identifying supporting evidence and matching it to the opinion it supports.
- Teach selecting the strongest authored evidence and a bounded evidence-connection explanation.

## Grade 3 boundary

- Do not ask learners to analyze how an argument develops.
- Do not ask learners to compare claims and counterclaims.
- Do not ask learners to explain why one source is more credible.
- Do not ask learners to evaluate bias or source reliability.
