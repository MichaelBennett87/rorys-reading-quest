## Pattern and boundary review

The target set contains closed, open, vowel-consonant-e, vowel-team, r-controlled, and consonant-le chunks. It also uses transparent compound, prefix, base, and suffix clues where they help decoding. Display chunks reconstruct each complete written word, authored speech chunks are pronounceable rather than letter names, and every source sentence contains its target as a complete word.

The teaching language treats syllable patterns as flexible strategies. It does not state that every word has one mechanical division, that two consonants always force one split, or that morphological and syllable boundaries always match. `un | cover` versus `un | cov | er`, `work | table` versus `work | ta | ble`, and `trail | blazer` versus `trail | bla | zer` explicitly demonstrate the distinction.
