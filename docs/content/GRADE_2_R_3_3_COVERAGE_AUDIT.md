## Review and evidence notes

- Same-topic behavior: authored in the informational pairs
- Same-theme behavior: authored in the literary pairs
- Similarity behavior: each checkpoint has at least one clear shared detail
- Difference behavior: each checkpoint has at least one clear contrast
- Important-detail behavior: minor details remain present but do not drive scoring
- Literary character and event behavior: characters and event order are compared where relevant
- Informational-detail behavior: central ideas, facts, and processes are compared where relevant
- Paired evidence behavior: both texts provide evidence for each comparison point
- Equal-plausibility behavior: checkpoints avoid two equally defensible comparison answers
