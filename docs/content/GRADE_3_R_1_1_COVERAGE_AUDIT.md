## Authored behavior

Character Arc Camp traces an initial character state, important middle events, response to a problem, turning point, changed strategy or understanding, and ending state. Guides and questions use actions, dialogue, thoughts, feelings, choices, and response-to-event evidence. Development explanations connect plot events to evidence from at least two stages, and checkpoints require beginning, middle, and end evidence.
