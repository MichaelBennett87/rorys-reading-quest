## Review checks

- Title review: each passage names the topic clearly and previews the article.
- Heading review: each section heading helps the reader locate or predict information.
- Caption review: each caption adds a helpful detail about the connected visual.
- Graph review: each graph organizes data for comparison and pattern noticing.
- Map review: each map shows locations, relationships, and routes with a fictional layout.
- Glossary review: each glossary explains words that may be unfamiliar in the passage.
- Illustration review: each illustration helps the reader picture a detail, object, or process.
- Body-feature connection review: the body text and features work together to add meaning.
- Feature-contribution review: every guide explains what the feature contributes, not just what it is.
- Graph-data consistency review: graph values are internally consistent and nonnegative.
- Fictional-map consistency review: map layout is authored, stable, and non-geographic.
- Glossary-definition review: glossary terms and definitions are clear and age-appropriate.
- Illustration-description review: illustration descriptions are original and readable.
- TextFeatureGuide review: each guide matches the passage, feature kinds, and content version.
- Evidence-reference review: sentence and feature evidence ids resolve safely.
- Central-idea exclusion review: no central-idea benchmark target is scored.
- Author-purpose exclusion review: no informational author-purpose benchmark target is scored.
- Author-opinion exclusion review: no opinion-and-evidence benchmark target is scored.
- Factuality review: passage facts are stable, age-appropriate, and internally consistent.
- Originality declaration: all passages, features, and questions are original.
- Copyright review: no protected informational passage, diagram, map, caption, or illustration was copied.
- Age-appropriateness review: content is child-safe and Grade 2 appropriate.
- Answer-choice review: answer choices are distinct and defensible.
- Explanation review: every question includes a concise child-friendly explanation.
- Word-help review: curated support targets stay on body sentences and do not expose feature answers early.
- Accessibility review: structure remains semantic, keyboard-operable, and readable.
