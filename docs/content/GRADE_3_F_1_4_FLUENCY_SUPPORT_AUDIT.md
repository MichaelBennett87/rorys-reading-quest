## Standard boundary

`ELA.3.F.1.4` calls for grade-level reading with accuracy, automaticity, and appropriate prosody or expression. The application cannot directly measure those oral behaviors because it does not record or analyze speech.

## Measurement limitations

Question correctness means only that the learner identified an authored fluent-reading choice from visible text. It is not evidence of oral reading accuracy, automaticity, pronunciation, expression, prosody, or rate.

The application does not provide:

- microphone access
- speech recognition
- audio recording
- words-correct-per-minute measurement or estimate
- pronunciation scoring
- prosody scoring
- expression scoring
- oral accuracy scoring
- oral fluency mastery
- FAST prediction
- global grade-level diagnosis

Model listening uses optional browser `SpeechSynthesis`. Voice quality and expression vary by browser and installed voice. The text and visual phrase cues remain authoritative, and no question depends on hearing audio.
